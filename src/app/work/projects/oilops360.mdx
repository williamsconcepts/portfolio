## Overview

OilOps360 is a digital operations compliance platform designed to help oil and gas organizations manage contractor compliance, asset inspections, operational records, and document recovery processes. The MVP transforms manual workflows such as paper documentation, spreadsheets, and scattered files into a centralized digital workspace.

## Problem Statement

Oil and gas operations depend on accurate compliance records, equipment inspections, and operational documentation to maintain safety, reliability, and regulatory standards. However, many organizations still rely on paper-based documentation, spreadsheets, email communication, manual inspection records, and scattered compliance files. This creates challenges such as difficulty tracking contractor compliance documents, expired certifications, poor visibility into asset conditions, delayed reporting, increased compliance risks, and accidental loss of critical records.

## Solution Overview

OilOps360 provides a centralized digital workflow for managing contractor compliance records, asset inspection activities, operational reports, deleted record recovery, and compliance visibility dashboards. This MVP demonstrates how traditional operational processes can be digitized before integrating a full backend infrastructure.

## Core Features

### 1. Contractor Compliance Management

The contractor module allows organizations to manage third-party compliance information, including:

- Register contractors
- Track compliance status
- Manage required documents
- Search contractor records
- Filter compliance conditions
- Export compliance reports

Tracked documents include:

- HSE certificates
- Insurance documents
- Tax clearance
- Company registration
- Safety certifications

### 2. Asset Inspection Management

The asset management module helps operations teams monitor equipment health and inspection schedules, including:

- Register operational assets
- Record inspection information
- Track inspection history
- Monitor asset condition
- Search and filter assets
- Export inspection reports

Supported assets include:

- Pumps
- Generators
- Storage tanks
- Wellheads
- Valves
- Processing equipment

### 3. Document Recovery & Data Protection

OilOps360 prevents permanent loss of important operational records through a recovery workflow. If a compliance manager accidentally deletes HSE certificates, inspection reports, or asset records, the system moves them to recovery storage instead of removing them permanently, maintaining audit history and allowing restoration when needed.

## Technical Approach

The MVP focuses on a lightweight frontend-first implementation with browser-based storage and basic workflow management, making it suitable for demonstrating the concept before full backend integration.

## Business Value

OilOps360 helps organizations strengthen compliance visibility, reduce paperwork, improve inspection tracking, and protect critical operational records.
